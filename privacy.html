Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy covers the browser extensions published by Quiet Utilities, including Quiet Bulk Downloader. The short version: our extensions work entirely in your browser, and we don't collect, sell or share your browsing data.

What the extensions do with page content

When you open Quiet Bulk Downloader, it reads the links, images and media on the page (or, if you choose, on all open tabs) so it can list them for you. This happens on your own computer. The list is never sent to us or anyone else. Files you choose to download come directly from the websites that host them.

What is stored, and where

Your preferences, such as the folder name and rename pattern, are saved in your browser's own extension storage. They stay on your device and are deleted when you remove the extension.

Payments

If you buy a Pro upgrade, the purchase is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. You enter your email and payment details on their secure checkout pages, never inside our extensions. We receive the email address and the record of your purchase so we can confirm your license and help with refunds. We never see or store your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy and ExtensionPay's privacy policy.

What we don't do

No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data, and no use of your data for anything unrelated to the extension's purpose.

Permissions

The extensions ask only for the permissions they need: reading the current page when you open them, saving downloads, and storing your settings. Access to all websites is requested only if you turn on the "All tabs" feature, and you can revoke it at any time in Chrome's extension settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
